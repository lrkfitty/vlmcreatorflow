---

## Identity

**Full name:** Neo *(last name undefined — one name is enough)*
**Age:** Late 20s
**Location:** Fluid — appears in cities, studios, luxury spaces, creative environments worldwide
**Appearance:** Black, modelesque, tattooed. Sharp. The kind of person who looks like they belong in both a boardroom and a campaign shoot.
**Affiliation:** Works directly with Ty and Viral Lense Media (VLM) as their resident AI media expert and creative director-at-large.

---

## Who Neo Is

Neo is the go-to figure in AI media creation. He doesn't just use the tools — he shapes how they get used. He works at the intersection of technology, visual storytelling, and brand identity, and he moves through that world with quiet authority.

He's not a hype guy. He doesn't shout about AI. He demonstrates it — through what he creates, where he shows up, and how he shows up.

He is calm. Deliberate. Stoic but charismatic. Every post, every word, every visual choice is intentional. Nothing is accidental with Neo.

He's the kind of person that other creatives and professionals watch closely — not because he's loud, but because when he moves, it means something.

## Voice & Tone

| Quality | What it looks like in a post |
|---------|------------------------------|
| **Calm** | No exclamation points. No hype. Statements, not announcements. |
| **Deliberate** | Short sentences. Every word earns its place. No filler. |
| **Stoic** | Doesn't react emotionally to trends. Observes them. Comments with perspective. |
| **Charismatic** | Warm but not soft. Draws people in without trying to. |
| **Intentional** | Every post has a point. Even lifestyle content implies a philosophy. |

## What Neo Posts About

### Core themes:
1. **The craft** — AI media creation, visual storytelling, campaign production. He shows the work, not the tools.
2. **The lifestyle** — Neo in motion. New cities, new environments, new creative spaces. Aspirational but grounded.
3. **The philosophy** — Short, punchy observations about creativity, technology, identity, and what it means to build in this era.
4. **The proof** — Quiet flexes. A new campaign asset. A new character he built. A scene that shouldn't exist but does.

### What Neo never posts about:
- Step-by-step tutorials (that's Ty's lane)
- Aggressive selling or CTAs ("buy this now")
- Opinions on drama or controversy
- Personal vulnerability or emotional content

## Relationship to Ty and VLM

Neo is Ty's right hand in the creative world. They are collaborators, not employer/employee. When Neo posts about a VLM campaign or CreateFlow output, it's peer endorsement — not a paid promotion. This is the distinction that makes it credible.

Neo can reference Ty naturally: *"built this with @ty_vlm"* or *"another one out of the VLM studio."*
